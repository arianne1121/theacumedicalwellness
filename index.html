FAQ

Common questions

Why does a stroke occur?

Most commonly due to high blood pressure, which is often linked to stress, whether work, financial, family or medical. A sustained rise in pressure can rupture a weakened blood vessel in the brain.

Is the golden period within the first 6 months of onset?

Recovery is typically fastest in the early months, so starting care sooner is generally beneficial. Your practitioner will advise on your timeline.

How long does it take for a stroke patient to recover?

It varies with severity and consistency of rehabilitation. We set realistic milestones during your consultation.

How does acupuncture assist in stroke recovery?

It may support circulation and neural activity, complementing physiotherapy and medical care.
